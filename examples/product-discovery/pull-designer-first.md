Fold is exploring how small design studios get a clear client sign-off without making the client join a whole project workspace. The PM's working hypothesis is that feedback and approval get mixed together. That hasn't been validated with customers.

The initial pilot direction is a review link for each uploaded version. A client must explicitly approve the version they reviewed; opening it doesn't count. These are decisions and requirements, not built features. The broader workspace was deferred to keep the experiment small.

For design, the useful distinction is between seeing the work and deliberately accepting it. How clients get access is still open, so an account-based entry flow would be an assumption. The PM owns that decision.

Side-by-side comparison is a PM-owned proposal, not committed scope or a proven need. A scripted walkthrough motivated the approval distinction; it isn't customer research. You have enough direction to explore the flow, but this record doesn't assign you work or settle its detailed UI.
