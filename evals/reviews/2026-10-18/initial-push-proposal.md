## Proposed shared record

# Fold

Fold is an unbuilt early concept for small design studios reviewing work with clients. It explores whether clearer sign-off helps when feedback, viewing and approval get mixed together. That problem remains a working hypothesis: no studios have been interviewed.

## Decisions

- D1 — Working decision — Use a separate review link for each uploaded version as the starting direction. Source: PM session01; approved project brief in session02.
- D2 — Decided — Defer the broader client workspace from the pilot direction. Projects and chat would widen the pilot before the explicit-approval idea is tested. This is a deferral, not a permanent rejection. Source: PM session01 and approved brief.

## Requirements

- R1 — Clients must explicitly approve the version they reviewed. Opening the design does not count as approval. Source: PM session02 and approved brief.

## Open Questions

- Q1 — How will clients get access: accounts or link-based access? The PM will settle access before pilot scope is ready. Source: PM sessions01–02 and approved brief.

## Proposals
