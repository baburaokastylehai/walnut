# Confirmation policy

Apply confirmation per candidate.

Confirm both what the source supports and its status. Preserving that a human is considering an idea does not confirm the idea as true or adopted. Useful tentative context can be durable without becoming a Decision or Requirement.

## Publishable settled knowledge

A candidate may become durable project knowledge when either:

- a human participant asserted it as settled fact, an instruction, a rejection, or a direction they subsequently treated as settled; or
- a current authoritative project source states it as current truth.

Assistant-originated ideas do not become project truth unless a human later adopts them.

Partial acceptance means only the accepted portion qualifies.

Ambiguity defaults to exclusion.

## Proposals and hypotheses

Preserve meaningful human-owned proposals or hypotheses explicitly under consideration, labeled as tentative or untested. A human actively asking to explore, compare or evaluate an idea establishes that exploration as theirs, including when the idea originated with an assistant. It does not establish the idea as a chosen direction or its assumptions as facts.

Merely hearing an assistant suggestion, acknowledging the response, or continuing the conversation does not establish adoption or ownership. Exclude unowned assistant speculation. When ownership or status is unclear, do not guess.

## Open questions

An Open Question qualifies when:
- a human genuinely raised it;
- it is still unresolved;
- it was not rhetorical, sarcastic, or merely operational chatter about Walnut itself.

A disagreement between human participants that remains unresolved is also an Open Question.

If it is later resolved, it should no longer remain active as an open question.

## Rejections

Explicit rejection is durable knowledge.

Record what was rejected and preserve the human/source reasoning when it exists. Do not invent rationale.

Distinguish permanent rejection from deferral or exclusion from current scope. Preserve a supplied reason or revisit condition without inventing a deadline, owner or future task. A deferred option has not become a commitment to implement it later.

## Third-party material

Pasted emails, research excerpts, specs, or quotes are evidence, not automatically decisions.

Place them under Research & Findings unless the human separately adopts a conclusion.

Preserve source, observations and stated limits. Evidence introduced for evaluation does not validate a hypothesis or establish a general conclusion by itself.

## Project overview synthesis

Walnut may synthesize the Project Overview from multiple confirmed facts when the resulting wording introduces no new factual claim.

Composition is allowed. Invention is not.

## Trust rule

Sparse and trustworthy is better than comprehensive and partly fabricated.
