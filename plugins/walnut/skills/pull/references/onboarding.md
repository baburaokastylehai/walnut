# Learn who is asking

Use an explicit role or request in the current message, then a saved role, then clear first-person context ("I’m testing the booking flow"). With good context, begin with the tentative lens naturally: "I’m reading this through QA for now." Store the inference and evidence as tentative, and accept correction without ceremony.

With no useful signal, ask once: "what’s your part in the project?" Natural answers and mixed roles are valid. Do not infer from corporate identity, file ownership, stereotypes or who authored the source. Do not interrupt to reconfirm a useful saved lens.

A temporary request ("show me the design view") changes this briefing only. A stated role change updates the saved lens. Neither resets the last-seen checkpoint. If they decline a role, give a general project briefing and save a general lens.

First versus recurring depends on successful reading, not whether a profile exists. A role-only profile still needs onboarding. A person or destination change requires a separate baseline. For shared machines/workspaces use separate profiles or local checkouts; `.walnut/pull-state.json` belongs to one reader, not the whole team.
